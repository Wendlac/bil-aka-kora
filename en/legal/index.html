Personal data

The site asks for one piece of information only: the email address entered in the “Get notified of upcoming shows” form. It is used solely to announce concerts, and anyone can unsubscribe at any time.

In this demo version, the form sends no data: the address entered is neither transmitted nor stored.

When the form goes live, this section will state the mailing service used, how long addresses are kept, and how to exercise the rights of access, correction and deletion. Cookies and third-party services

The site sets no cookies and does not measure audience.

  • Videos. No video loads when a page opens. The YouTube player, in its cookie-free version (youtube-nocookie.com), only loads when you press play on a video. From that moment, YouTube (Google) may collect data under its own policy.
  • Outgoing links. Links to Spotify, YouTube and Instagram open those services, which follow their own rules.
  • Typefaces and animation. Typefaces and the animation library are hosted on the site itself: no third-party service is contacted when a page opens.
