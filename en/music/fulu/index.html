Album, 2019

Fulu

  • 19 December 2019
  • 11 tracks
  • 39 min
  • Shamar Empire, Djongo Diffusion

On Fulu, Bil Aka Kora looks squarely at his time. The title track tells of a woman or man reaching middle age after a youth spent enjoying everything except choosing someone to love.

Sung in Dioula and Kassena, Atikeleye calls for care of the land as much as of human values. Mani, in Italian and Dioula, speaks of the everyday violence we all have to face.
